3.1 Metric preliminaries

Definition 18
#

Lean declarations

  • MetricSpaces.deletedBall

For a metric space \((X,d)\), \(x \in X\), and \(r {\gt} 0\):

\[ B(x,r)=\{ y\in X : d(x,y){\lt}r\} , \]
\[ \overline{B}(x,r)=\{ y\in X : d(x,y)\le r\} , \]
\[ B^\ast (x,r)=\{ y\in X : 0{\lt}d(x,y){\lt}r\} . \]
Definition 19
#

Lean declarations

  • MetricSpaces.sequenceBounded

The basic notions are encoded as:

\[ E \text{ bounded } \iff \exists x\in X\ \exists R{\gt}0,\ E \subseteq B(x,R), \]
\[ p \text{ is a limit point of } E \iff \forall r{\gt}0,\ \bigl(B^\ast (p,r)\cap E\bigr)\neq \varnothing , \]
\[ (x_n) \text{ bounded } \iff \exists x\in X\ \exists R{\gt}0,\ \forall n,\ x_n \in B(x,R). \]
